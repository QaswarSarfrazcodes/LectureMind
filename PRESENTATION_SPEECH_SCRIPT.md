### Part 1: The Problem & Introduction [0:00 – 0:25]
*(Action: Facecam or showing [https://qaswarsarfrazcodes.github.io/LectureMind/](https://qaswarsarfrazcodes.github.io/LectureMind/) on screen)*

> **"Hello judges and developers! My name is Qaswar Sarfraz, and this is LectureMind — an autonomous, voice-first academic synthesizer built for university students.**

That is exactly why I built LectureMind."**

### Part 2: Live Recording & Voice Ingestion [0:25 – 0:55]
*(Action: Click the glowing red 'Record Session' button at `http://localhost:8080/#/record`. Speak a quick sample or let the live audio stream run for 10-15 seconds)*

> **"Let's see it in action. I'll hit Record Session. LectureMind captures audio directly in the browser and connects to AssemblyAI's state-of-the-art Universal-2 speech recognition engine.**
> 
> **Whether it's complex operating system scheduling, algorithms, or medical biology, AssemblyAI transcribes spoken lecture audio with unmatched technical vocabulary accuracy and sub-second latency.**
> 
> **Now, I'll stop the recording and click 'Process Lecture'. LectureMind initiates parallel synthesis — classifying academic domain, extracting pedagogical pillars, and constructing an interconnected knowledge graph."**

---

### Part 3: The 100% Speech Mind Map & Visual Synthesis [0:55 – 1:40]
*(Action: The Notes Detail screen opens. Click directly on the 'Mind Map' tab. Pan and zoom around the canvas with your mouse, then click 'Download JPG')*

> **"Here is where LectureMind completely sets itself apart. Look at this Mind Map.**
> 
> **Unlike generic summarizers that drop 80% of what you say, LectureMind's synthesizer utilizes 100% of the speech transcript. Every meaningful proposition spoken by the professor is mapped into a 4-tier conceptual hierarchy:**
> 
> **At Tier 0, we have the Central Academic Subject. Radiating outwards at Tier 1 are the Core Thematic Pillars derived directly from the speech topics.**
> 
> **At Tier 2, we have precise Sub-Concepts, and branching into Tier 3 are concrete Mechanism and Evidence leaves.**
> 
> **Notice these golden amber nodes? Those are Domain Expansion nodes — automatically synthesized to connect the lecture to real-world industry standards. And with a single click on 'Download JPG', students can export high-resolution study diagrams for exam revision."**

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording Your Video

1. **Audio Quality:** Use your earphones/headset mic so your voice is crisp and clear.
2. **Screen Resolution:** Keep your browser at 1080p (Full HD) so the Mind Map nodes and text are razor-sharp.
3. **Pacing:** Don't rush! Let each click show on screen for 1–2 seconds before transitioning.
4. **Local URL:** Record on `http://localhost:8080` (loads in 0.1s with no network delay).
5. **Backup Demo Lecture Text to Speak (if speaking live):**
   > *"Today we are discussing Database Management Systems and Relational Normalization. In First Normal Form (1NF), we eliminate repeating groups and ensure atomic column values. Second Normal Form (2NF) removes partial dependencies on composite primary keys. Third Normal Form (3NF) eliminates transitive dependencies where non-key attributes depend on other non-key attributes. Finally, transaction integrity is governed by ACID properties: Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability."*
