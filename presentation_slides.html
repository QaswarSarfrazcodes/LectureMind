🚨 The Problem

The Note-Taking Paradox

During 60 to 90-minute university lectures, students face an impossible cognitive split: either write verbatim notes OR listen deeply to comprehend — rarely both.

90%

Terminology Hallucination

Standard STT tools scramble complex academic terminology (formulas, algorithms, economics terms).

0%

Passive Flat Summaries

Existing tools produce unstructured walls of passive text that students skim without retaining.

75%

Study Material Friction

Students spend hours formatting notes and creating diagrams instead of revising for exams.

💡 The Solution

LectureMind Studio

An integrated 5-pillar cognitive workstation designed in a Neumorphic Dark Navy aesthetic, turning acoustic speech into exam-ready visual & conceptual assets.

🎙️ Acoustic Voice Stream

Low-latency streaming to AssemblyAI with specialized academic vocabulary boosting.

🗺️ 4-Tier Visual Mind Map

Interactive hierarchical node graph (Central → Core → Subtopics → Leaves) with 1-tap JPG export.

🧠 Socratic Bloom's Quiz

🏗️ Architecture & Technology

Client-First Multi-Model Pipeline

Zero-backend server footprint. Ultra-fast direct streaming from Flutter Web and Mobile to AssemblyAI & Groq.
